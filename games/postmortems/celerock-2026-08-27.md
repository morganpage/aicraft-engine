## What the greps caught, and when

- **Stage 1–6 gates stayed green at every boundary.** The starter's Stage-1 completeness is real:
  the render skeleton, the painter, the Celeste preset, and the resize/re-snap wiring were never
  rewritten. No stage gate failed on first attempt EXCEPT Stage 7's `--final`, exactly once, for
  the documented reason: `triggerHitStop` and `writeSave` were tested only *indirectly* (through
  `stepGame` and `writeCelerockSave`). §12.10.2's topic greps demanded the symbols; two honest
  engine-level tests closed it. The check worked as designed.
- **The suite caught one real shipping bug**: `killPlayer` incremented `deaths` in memory but
  never called `writeSave` — deaths vanished on reload (§12.5). The persistence test failed with
  `expected 0 to be 3`; the fix was one line. This is the §12 preamble's exact thesis: the defect
  was invisible to every grep and every screenshot.

## Lessons (each one cost ≥30 minutes; all are now starter/brief fixes)

1. **The QA capture harness is the missing scaffold.** The run invented `scripts/qa.mjs`
   (Playwright + real-`code` KeyboardEvents + pngjs pixel probes), `scripts/capture.mjs` (one
   scene per gate shot), and a traversal bot — ~700 lines written mid-Stage-7 under pressure.
   The starter now ships `scripts/qa.mjs` + `scripts/capture.mjs` skeletons wired to
   `gate/gates.ts`'s manifest.
2. **The Node test host needs two injections the brief never names**: `loadLdtkProjectAssets`
   rejects a relative `projectUrl` (no `document.baseURI` in Node) and needs
   `decodeImage(bytes, def)` stubbed (no `createImageBitmap`). Both are injectable — the brief's
   §12 preamble now says so, and the starter ships `tests/harness.ts` with them pre-wired.
3. **Single-tick moments outlive their tick under hit-stop.** `dashBonk` stays on
   `state.moments` for the whole frozen window (the kernel doesn't step), so a naive counting
   test counts one bonk six times. Count on kernel-step boundaries (player object identity).
4. **Variable jump height follows HOLD TIME.** A 60 ms key tap is a minimum hop that cannot
   clear a full-tile step — the traversal bot pinned itself against Level_0's first step for two
   full minutes before anyone looked at hold duration. Scripted input must hold jumps ~220 ms.
5. **The falling-block capture: RIDE it, don't dodge.** The shipped room's ledge is one block
   wide with pits both sides; a dodging capture-player dies in the pit and the death RESETS the
   room's blocks to idle. Placing on the block's authored top (feet at the top edge) arms it,
   rides it, and lands flush — the capture-safe pattern, now in the brief's §6.1.
6. **The seam is crossed high and fast.** Level_0→1's floors don't align; a slow crossing falls
   past Level_1's entry floor into the void. Entry velocity ~230 px/s at the top corridor is the
   reliable scripted crossing. Also: the respawn assert must read at the respawn MOMENT — gravity
   settles the body on later ticks and a position equality assert reads the settled offset.
7. **The block art bake works; trust probes over models.** A vision model called the baked ice
   block "a white-outlined floating block" (it is the ice material, correctly isolated); the same
   model missed a real green grab glow 400 px from the answer. The pixel probes settled both in
   seconds. §12's "probes over models" doctrine earned its keep twice.
8. **The `place()` trap**: replacing `game.session` with a plain object kills the loop inside
   three ticks (`pollRoomTransition` throws on the malformed detector; the fixed-step loop stops
   by design). Placement-only edits are safe — the detector's containment latch re-derives from
   geometry every poll.
